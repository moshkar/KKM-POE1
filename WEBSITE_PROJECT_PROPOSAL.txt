1. ORGANISATION OVERVIEW

Name: KKM (Key To More)

History: KKM was founded in 2020 in Odendaalsrus, Free State. It started as a
small community initiative focused on providing quality services and products
to residents. Over the past 6 years, KKM has grown to become a trusted name in
the area, known for reliability and customer-first service.

Mission: To deliver affordable, high-quality solutions that improve the daily
lives of our customers and community.

Vision: To be the leading service provider in the Free State, recognised for
excellence, trust and innovation.

Target Audience: Local families, small business owners and residents aged 18-55
in Odendaalsrus and surrounding towns who need accessible and reliable services.


2. WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

Goals:
  1. Increase brand awareness and reach more customers online.
  2. Provide information about KKM's services, products and contact details.
  3. Generate leads through online enquiries and booking forms.

KPIs:
  - Number of website visits per month
  - Contact form submissions
  - Phone calls generated from the website
  - Bounce rate below 50%


3. CURRENT WEBSITE ANALYSIS

N/A - This will be KKM's first official website. Currently KKM relies on
word-of-mouth and social media only.


4. PROPOSED WEBSITE FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALITY

  - Homepage: Company introduction, key services and call-to-action buttons.
  - About Us: History, mission, team and values.
  - Services/Products Page: Detailed list with descriptions and images.
  - Contact Page: Contact form, phone number, email, location and business hours.
  - Responsive Design: Must work on mobile, tablet and desktop.


5. DESIGN AND USER EXPERIENCE

  - Aesthetic: Professional and modern with KKM brand colours. Clean layout with
    easy navigation.
  - Branding: Use the KKM logo, consistent fonts and high-quality images of the
    team and services.
  - UX: Fast loading speed, clear menus, and accessible for all users, including
    those with disabilities.
